# VISION.md
## Ultimate Goal
To create a fully autonomous, 24/7 continuous TV show generator that transforms real-world audio conversations into stylized, rendered, and published sitcom episodes without human intervention.

## Core Foundational Concepts
- **Continuous Ingestion:** Always listening, chunking, and transcribing real-world audio.
- **Intelligent Distillation:** Finding the humor, absurdity, or narrative arc in mundane conversations.
- **Automated Creativity:** Transforming distilled seeds into highly structured, lore-compliant sitcom scripts.
- **Programmatic Rendering:** Constructing audio (TTS) and visual (compositing) representations automatically.
- **Frictionless Publishing:** Automatically syndicating generated content to major social networks (YouTube, TikTok, X).

## User-Satisfaction Design
- The system must require zero manual operation once started.
- All pipeline stages must be completely transparent via a single, intuitive Dashboard UI.
- High-value interactions (viewing generated scripts, observing render progress) must be prominently displayed.
- The UI must combine parallel concepts on a single page to eliminate subpage navigation.
