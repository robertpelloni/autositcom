# Style Guide

## Tone & Voice
- The tone should be similar to classic 90s sitcoms with a modern, slightly absurd twist.
- Characters should be exaggerated but grounded in their core obsessions.
- Fast-paced, punchy dialogue with overlapping arguments.

## Scene Structure
- **Setup:** Establish the core conflict immediately.
- **Escalation:** Characters double down on their absurd positions.
- **Punchline/Resolution:** A sudden realization or ridiculous compromise.

## Formatting
- Each scene must occur in one specific `setting`.
- Dialogue should clearly label the `speaker` and their `line`.
